### Content Blocks

#### I.1 — Welcome

> **Welcome to the Manager Audit**
>
> Thank you for participating in the Invincibility Blueprint™ — your organisation's operational diagnostic.
>
> As a manager, you have direct visibility into how your department operates day to day. Your input is essential to understanding what works, what doesn't, and where the real bottlenecks are.
>
> The Invincibility Blueprint is a fast, senior-led diagnostic that reveals how the organisation actually operates, identifies where execution breaks down, and defines a prioritised route to stronger performance. It is a system review — not a performance evaluation of individuals.

#### I.2 — What this audit covers

> **What You'll Be Asked About**
>
> The Manager Audit focuses on your department's operational reality. You will be asked about:
>
> - **Your role** — title, department, team size, reporting line
> - **Role purpose** — what "good" looks like, how success is measured
> - **Regular duties** — daily/weekly cadence, recurring tasks, time allocation
> - **Day-in-the-life** — how a typical day unfolds, time sinks, interruptions
> - **Systems and portals** — tools you use, what works, what creates friction
> - **Throughput blockers** — what slows your team down, recurring problems
> - **Department-specific operations** — questions tailored to your function
> - **Non-productive time** — where time is lost to rework, waiting, admin
> - **Cost leakage** — visible waste, unnecessary spend, hidden costs
> - **People management** — team capability, training, retention
> - **Interfaces and handoffs** — where your work connects with other departments
> - **Reality test** — rating scales on key operational dimensions

#### I.3 — What to expect

> **What to Expect**
>
> | | |
> |---|---|
> | **Estimated time** | 30–45 minutes |
> | **Sections** | 13 sections (some tailored to your department) |
> | **Format** | Free-text answers, dropdowns, and rating scales |
> | **Saving** | Your progress is saved automatically — you can leave and return at any time |
> | **After submission** | A 60-minute Manager Interview will follow to explore themes and fill any gaps |
>
> **Department routing:** After you select your department in Section 1, the audit will include a section with questions specific to your function (e.g., Field Ops, Dispatch, Stores, QA, HSEQ, HR).
>
> You do not need to complete this in one sitting. Each section saves independently.

#### I.4 — Key principles

> **Before You Begin**
>
> - **This is a system review, not a blame exercise.** We are looking at how the organisation operates — not evaluating you or your team.
> - **Honesty is more valuable than perfection.** Best estimates and candid observations are far more useful than carefully worded answers.
> - **There are no wrong answers.** If a question doesn't apply to your role, say so. If you're unsure, give your best estimate.
> - **Your responses are confidential.** Individual responses are used to identify themes and patterns — they are not shared verbatim with your leadership.

### Design Notes

- Purely informational landing step with no interactive elements and no data to save.
- The "Save and continue" button is enabled immediately (gating happens at the next step, Consent).
- Visual treatment: guidance cards with amber/orange left border, consistent with the Consent step styling.
- The progress sidebar shows "Introduction" as the first item, highlighted as active.
- Slightly more concise than the Company Audit intro — managers are mid-level participants with less time.
- The mention of "department routing" in I.3 prepares the participant for the conditional branching in Section 5, reducing surprise.
- On return visits, the wizard may skip directly to the last incomplete section, but Introduction remains accessible via the sidebar.
