### Content Blocks

#### I.1 — Welcome

> **Welcome to the Invincibility Blueprint™ Diagnostic**
>
> Thank you for taking the time to complete this Company Audit as part of the Invincibility Blueprint™ — your organisation's operational diagnostic.
>
> This is the first step in building a clear, evidence-based picture of how your organisation operates today and where it can be strengthened.

#### I.2 — What is the Invincibility Blueprint

> **About the Invincibility Blueprint™**
>
> The Invincibility Blueprint is a fast, senior-led diagnostic process that:
>
> - Reveals how your organisation actually operates day to day
> - Identifies where execution breaks down — from strategy through to field delivery
> - Defines a clear, prioritised route to stronger performance
>
> It combines self-submission audits (like this one), structured interviews, and AI-powered synthesis to produce actionable findings and recommendations.

#### I.3 — What this audit covers

> **What This Audit Covers**
>
> The Company Audit is the broadest of the three diagnostic instruments. As the senior leader, your submission provides the organisational-level evidence that frames everything else.
>
> You will be asked about:
>
> - **Organisational Foundation** — purpose, values, operating system, authority matrix
> - **Structure & Roles** — organograms, headcount, workforce mix
> - **Workforce & Performance** — KPIs, training, retention, career pathways
> - **Strategy & Planning** — business plans, budgets, forecasts, risk registers
> - **Process & Compliance** — SOPs, checklists, safety, quality systems
> - **Operating Cadence** — meetings, reporting rhythms, decision cycles
> - **External Systems** — client portals, scheduling tools, CRMs
> - **Finance & Cost** — P&L, cost breakdowns, throughput analysis
> - **Operations Deep Dive** — installs, service, pre-enablement, enablement, dispatch, stores, QA, HSEQ
> - **Leadership Signals** — management visibility, communication, recognition
> - **Evidence Register** — supporting documents you can upload

#### I.4 — What to expect

> **What to Expect**
>
> | | |
> |---|---|
> | **Estimated time** | 60–90 minutes |
> | **Sections** | 11 content sections + evidence register |
> | **Format** | Mix of free-text answers, structured tables, and document uploads |
> | **Saving** | Your progress is saved automatically — you can leave and return at any time |
> | **After submission** | A 90-minute Director Interview will follow to explore themes and fill any gaps |
>
> You do not need to complete this in one sitting. Each section saves independently, so you can work through it over several sessions.

#### I.5 — Key principles

> **Before You Begin**
>
> A few important things to keep in mind:
>
> - **This is a system review, not a blame exercise.** We are looking at how the organisation operates, not evaluating individuals.
> - **Evidence over polish.** Raw exports, working drafts, and screenshots are more useful than polished presentations. Submit what you already have, in whatever format you have it.
> - **Incomplete is OK.** Most organisations don't have every document fully developed — that's normal. Gaps are filled through interviews and follow-ups.
> - **Speed and truth matter more than perfection.** Best estimates and honest answers are far more valuable than carefully curated responses.
> - **For each document request, you can choose:** Upload what you have / Provide a link / Mark as "not available yet" / Mark as "will send later".

### Design Notes

- This is a purely informational landing step with no interactive elements and no data to save.
- The "Save and continue" button is enabled immediately (no gating — gating happens at the next step, Consent).
- Visual treatment: each content block is rendered as a guidance card (consistent with the amber/orange left-border cards used in Consent).
- The progress sidebar shows "Introduction" as the first item, highlighted as active.
- On return visits where the participant has already passed this step, the wizard may optionally skip directly to the last incomplete section (existing resume behaviour), but Introduction remains accessible via the sidebar.
- Mobile considerations: content blocks should stack cleanly on smaller screens. The section overview list (I.3) may use a collapsed/expandable pattern on mobile.
