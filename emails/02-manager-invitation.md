Hi [NAME],

As part of the Invincibility Blueprint® diagnostic, please complete the Managers' Audit for your area and upload any supporting evidence you already use day-to-day.

**Form link:** [MANAGER_FORM_LINK]
**Deadline:** [DEADLINE]
**Time required:** ~30–45 minutes (longer if you choose to upload more evidence)

### Before you start

- You are not expected to have everything documented — submit what you have in the format you have.
- Incomplete is OK. More evidence helps, but perfection is not required.
- Please don't rewrite documents — raw exports, screenshots and working trackers are best.

### How to submit

1. Open the form and select your department/team.
2. Answer the sections tagged "Applies to" your role (type "N/A" where not relevant).
3. Upload your "Regular Duties & Cadence" list for your area (or the closest substitute: rota, meeting cadence, supervisor tracker, checklists, SOP index).
4. If you work across both the internal FSM and client portals, include any examples of portal steps, rejection reasons, exception lists, or reconciliation trackers (screenshots are fine).
5. Submit — if anything is missing, we'll pick it up in interview.
