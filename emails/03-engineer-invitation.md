## Email Body

Hi [NAME],

We're running the Invincibility Blueprint® diagnostic to remove avoidable waste, rework and friction from day-to-day delivery. We're asking a small sample of engineers to complete a short mini-audit.

**Survey link:** [ENGINEER_FORM_LINK]
**Deadline:** [DEADLINE]
**Time required:** ~10–12 minutes

### What we need from you

- Your view of where time is lost (travel, stores, waiting, admin/portals, repeat visits)
- What causes jobs to fail or rework to happen
- What would make the job easier and more productive

### Before you start

- This is not about blame. We're looking at system issues, not individuals.
- Please answer honestly. Short, direct answers are best.
- Don't include sensitive personal data.

### How to submit

1. Open the survey link.
2. Answer the questions from your experience (best estimate is fine).
3. If you have any examples (e.g., a screenshot of a common portal rejection reason, a typical "job pack" requirement, or a recurring failure note), you can upload it — optional.
4. Submit.

### Confidentiality and data handling

The Client is the data controller and [Supplier legal name] acts as a data processor on the Client's behalf. Information is handled confidentially and used only to produce the diagnostic findings and recommendations. Please do not include special category personal data unless strictly necessary.
